How the calculation works. The personal allowance (£12,570) is deducted from non-savings income first, and tapers away once income passes £100,000. Dividends are then stacked on top of taxable non-savings income. The first £500 is covered by the dividend allowance at 0%. The remainder is taxed at 8.75% within the basic-rate band (up to £37,700), 33.75% in the higher-rate band (to £125,140), and 39.35% above that. Figures are for the 2025/26 tax year and assume a standard UK taxpayer with no other reliefs.
